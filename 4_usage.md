# Using the Syringe Pumps

## Selecting the pump {pagestep}

- To **select or deselect** Pump A or B, press the corresponding letter (A or B) in red.
- When selected, a star (*) will appear on the left of the letter. 
- When deselected, the star (*) will disappear.

## Adjusting pump parameters {pagestep}

- Press the **enter (#) key** to choose a parameter:
     - `F` represents Flow Rate (in microliters per hour, **uL/h**).
     - `D` represents Syringe Diameter (in millimeters, **mm**).
- Select the pump (A or B) by pressing its letter in red:
     - A star (*) will appear on the left of the letter, indicating it is ready for modification.
- Enter the new value:
     - Press the **D key** to insert a decimal point (.) if needed.
- Press **enter (#) key** to save the value.
     - The letter will return to its original state, showing the new value.

## Running the pump {pagestep}

- To **start the pump**, select it and press the star (*) button.
- A greater-than symbol (>) will appear on the right of the letter, indicating that the pump is running.
- To **stop the pump**, select it and press the star (*) button again. The letter should return to its original state (without any symbol next to it).

## Modifying parameters while the pump is running {pagestep}
- When a pump is running (`>` symbol is displayed), you can still adjust the parameters.
- Press **(#)** to select the parameter (F or D), then select the pump, enter the new value, and press **enter (#)** to confirm.
- The pump will adjust its speed accordingly.
